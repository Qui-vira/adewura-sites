## What the parent page takes from it (17 Oct 2026)

- Loader: the word about 42% of the screen width and the object as tall as the word.
- The loader gives way to a cloud sky, and the welcome sits on that sky.
- "Scroll to explore": mouse inside a ring that fades out and back in, about 2.6 s per cycle.
- Scroll flies the camera forward through the clouds. Each house comes out of the clouds in turn (Ilé Àtègbè's beaded crown, the Trust Fund's maize, Adewura Natural's Restoria), then rushes past. Between houses the camera passes through a cloud bank, the white flash.

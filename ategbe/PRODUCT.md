# Product: Ilé Àtègbè

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People drawn to Ifá and Òrìṣà practice, from first-time seekers to initiates, many in the United States diaspora. They come for divination, prayers, education, initiation and ritual products, and need to trust the lineage first.

## Product Purpose

Present the lineage, its custodian and its services with the respect the tradition carries, and help a visitor book divination, join a class or contact the house.

## Positioning

Per ileategbe.org: Àtègbè derives from Ìretè and Ogbè, a chapter of the 256 Odù of the Ifá corpus, meaning "stepping into greatness". The lineage is traced over 500 years through Oba Òjó Ifá and Babaláwo Adédèjì to the current custodian, Dr. Ifábùkúnmi O. Adéwálé, born 1964, author of several books on Yorùbá divination and medicinal plants. Based in Conley, Georgia.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages in this build: Home, Our History and Custodian, Education (classes, 16 Major Odù Ifá, the Àkóṣe book), Iwure and Prayer Calendar, Contact. Services listed: Ifá divination, head rogation, receiving of Òrìṣà, initiation, Ẹgbẹ́ initiation in America.
- Hero object: the beaded Àtègbè crown, built in code with individual beads in the fringe, turning with scroll. Confirmed by the designer.
- Sacred content: the client approves how the crown, Odù signs and ancestors are shown before anything goes public.
- Yoruba diacritics must be correct everywhere. The old site has inconsistent spellings (Àtègbè vs ÀTẸ̀GBÈ, ÒRÌŞÀ with a Turkish Ş). Client to confirm the house spelling.

## Brand Commitments

- Signature hues: amber #FFB100 and crown indigo #301F93, with antique gold from the logo for ornament only.
- Logo: "ILÉ ÀTÈGBÈ" in carved gold letters. Keep or redesign is pending the client.

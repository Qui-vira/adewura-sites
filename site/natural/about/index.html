About Adewura Natural

Your trusted partner in natural remedies

Adewura Natural Medical Centre in Lagos, Nigeria, and Adewura Natural Remedies in the United States make remedies from plants rich in vitamins and minerals such as potassium, magnesium and zinc, which every living cell needs.

How we work

With communities

We support community health through health talks and advice.

With farmers

We work with local farmers to grow the plants our remedies are made from, which also creates jobs.

Next, we want to reach more people and communities, make our products easier to find, and keep them affordable.

Dr. Ifábùkúnmi O. Adéwálé

Medical director of Adewura Natural Medical Centre in Lagos, Nigeria, and Adewura Natural Remedies in the United States.

He comes from an extensive line of healers and trained under his father and uncle. He earned a Bachelor of Science in biology with special honors at the University of Lagos, trained as a biosystemic plant taxonomist and did postgraduate research in pharmacognosy.

His botanical garden holds over one thousand plant species and is a teaching site for ethnobotany students.

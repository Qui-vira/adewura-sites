The history of Ilé Àtègbè

Ilé Àtègbè can be summed up as the spiritual lineage that steps into greater spiritual heights.

The name

Àtègbè comes from Ìretè and Ogbè, one of the chapters of the 256 Odù of the Ifá literary corpus. Ilé means house, home, compound or lineage. Àtègbè means “stepping into greatness”, or “stepping into greater spiritual heights”.

This lineage has existed for thousands of years. Over the past five hundred, we trace the history of Ilé Àtègbè as follows.

I

Oba Òjó Ifá

More than five hundred years ago, Òjó Ifá was the Oba Àtègbè, the King of Àtègbè, and he ruled for about 150 years. He was so prolific an Oba that he reset the record of kingship for Ilé Àtègbè.

He was so spiritually tuned to Ifá that he did not have to throw the òpèlè (divination chain) to divine for his clients.1 He would simply hold his òpèlè in the air and look at the client through the opening. He would then place the òpèlè back in its container and begin to relay the message that Ifá had for them.

Oba Òjó Ifá made many predictions in his lifetime. One was that the thatched-roof houses they lived in would one day be spoken of as the kind of house built in the historic past. He predicted that the third king after him would continue his legacy, and would open the Ilé to the whole world through a small, box-like medium. Radio? Television? Computer?

He also predicted that the second king after him would be the bridge between the third king and himself. The third king would be born during the era of the second, and would be crowned at birth, so that the àṣẹ of Oba Òjó Ifá passed on to the newborn king.

II

Oba Adédèjì

The second king was Babaláwo Adédèjì, A Dafa Bí Eni Wejú: the crown becomes two, he divines as if he witnessed it happening. He earned the name through his lucidity in the Ifá divination system, a gift passed to him from Oba Òjó Ifá when he became king. Oba Adédèjì needed to throw his òpèlè only once to make a complete divination for a client or situation. In his royal priesthood he was so revered that he was also appointed diviner to other kings.

He was born with extraordinary physical qualities that resembled those of Orò, the Òrìṣà that prevents paralysis, and because of this he was directed to study Ifá. After he began that study, his life began to transform. On the Ìtá day of his initiation to Ifá, it was revealed that he was to be crowned the second king of Ilé Àtègbè. Since the reign of Oba Òjó Ifá, the position had been vacant for some three hundred years.

III

The third Oba

Oba Adédèjì passed away on December 26, 1976. His reign had ended sixteen years before, when the prediction Oba Òjó Ifá made more than five hundred years earlier came true. In 1964 the third Oba Àtègbè since Oba Òjó Ifá was born: our own Babaláwo Dr. Ifábùkúnmi Ònàolápò Adéwálé.

According to Oba Òjó Ifá, during this third reign the work of Ilé Àtègbè would carry the people five hundred years ahead of their time.

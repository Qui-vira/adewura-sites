What is Ifá divination?

Ifá is one of the sacred oral texts of the Yorùbá people of West Africa, in parts of present-day Nigeria, Benin and Burkina Faso. This ancient corpus holds the history, cosmology, worldview, language, medical tradition and ethical legacy of the Yorùbá people, preserved orally in poetic form.

Odù Ifá, our sacred oracle, is organized into 256 sections: 16 major Odù and 240 minor Odù, each holding countless verses. Traditionally the Odù are memorized and passed on orally by priests known as Babaláwo, who draw on the text during divination to guide people seeking advice and resolution for personal matters.

The Babaláwo uses the Ifá divination system to make a spiritual inquiry into one’s akashic record, to see how a problem or situation is best resolved. It is a means of communication between the natural and supernatural worlds, used as a guide to one’s destiny.

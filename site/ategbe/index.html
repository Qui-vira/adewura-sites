A house, a lineage

Àtègbè comes from Ìretè and Ogbè, one of the chapters of the 256 Odù of the Ifá literary corpus. Ilé means house, home, compound or lineage. Àtègbè means stepping into greatness, or stepping into greater spiritual heights.

More than five hundred years ago, Òjó Ifá was the Oba Àtègbè. He predicted that the third king after him would continue his legacy, and that the second would be the bridge between them.

The third Oba since Òjó Ifá

In 1964 the third Oba Àtègbè since Oba Òjó Ifá was born, as the old king had foretold: Babaláwo Dr. Ifábùkúnmi O. Adéwálé, custodian and head diviner of the house.

Born in Epòró, Emùré-Èkìtì, he is a practitioner and scholar of traditional Yorùbá medicine and religion, trained under his father and uncle and at the University of Lagos, and the author of Introduction to Ancient Yorùbá Divination Systems.

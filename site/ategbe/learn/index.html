The 16 major Odù Ifá

Here are the basic characteristics and patterns of the first six of the 16 major Odù. They follow the alternative arrangement, not because it is the best, but because it is the one most commonly used. Whether to follow the ancient orthodox or the alternative arrangement is a choice left to readers or to their spiritual lineage. Whichever is chosen, an entire divination must be completed using only one system of arrangement.

1

Èjì-Ogbè

The first of the basic 16 Odù, both in the ancient orthodox and the alternative type of arrangement.

Appearance
All 8 lobes of the Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ are open and marked on the Ọpọ́n-Ifá.
Pàtàkì (properties)

Individuals who receive this Odù will be presented with opportunities for fortune. In addition, he or she may be guided and directed to achieve new knowledge, will be successful in new ventures, and is seemingly bestowed with the advantage of “beginner’s luck”. Generosity, perseverance and hope are essential to the success of clients in difficult circumstances.

Clarity and the ability to assimilate knowledge, both spiritually and metaphysically, will at times make this individual seem to be clairvoyant. He or she will be “fertile”, meaning that most if not all of their ventures (family, business, spiritual, social) will prosper and flourish. It will seem to those around this individual that they have acquired the “goose that lays the golden eggs”. This is the blessing bestowed in Èjì-Ogbè that we commonly refer to as “beginner’s luck”, which allows a novice to accomplish a task successfully on their very first attempt. These opportunities stem from the term Ogbè, which means “to be blessed or supported”, and Èjì means double, so this client is doubly blessed.

In Èjì-Ogbè there is no room or tolerance for retaliation, because the Òrìṣà will clear one’s path of all obstacles and make one victorious. These verses emphasize the importance of forgiving wrongs done to one. Therefore, one should not quarrel or fight. One should also always attempt to be peaceful.

Peculiarity
Ọ̀rúnmìlà, Ọ̀ṣun, Orí, Olókun and Ìbejì are several of the Òrìṣà that speak very loudly in this Odù.
Color
All colors.

2

Ọ̀yèkú-Méjì

The second of the basic 16 Odù, both in the ancient orthodox and the alternative type of arrangement.

Appearance
All 8 lobes of the Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ are closed and marked on the Ọpọ́n-Ifá.
Pàtàkì (properties)

The literal translation of Ọ̀yèkú-Méjì is the ability to avert all negativities. Therefore, when this Odù is marked for the individual, all agents of death, sickness, disrespect and accompanying negativities will be neutralized and manifested in positive outcomes. These outcomes may include longevity, health, lucidity, and the ability to access the spiritual realm by dreaming.

Ọ̀yèkú-Méjì promotes cleanliness and the ability to acquire protectiveness, as well as prevention of disease and endemics.

Peculiarity
Òrìṣà that speak very loudly in this Odù are Ọ̀rúnmìlà, Egúngún, Ọbàtálá, Orò and Babalúayé.
Color
The favorite colors include, but are not limited to, varying shades of maroon.

3

Ìwòrì-Méjì

The third of the basic 16 Odù, both in the ancient orthodox and the alternative type of arrangement.

Appearance
The 1st and the 4th lobes of the Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ are closed while the 2nd and 3rd are open, marked on the Ọpọ́n-Ifá.
Pàtàkì (properties)

In Ìwòrì-Méjì, individuals are within the spiritual radii of surveillance and should remain cognizant that everything might be exposed. Therefore, these individuals need to exercise vigilance in all endeavors. They also have the natural ability and potential for skillfulness in most of their activities. These are attributes commonly associated with hunters, the military and armed forces, engineers, metallurgists and meteorologists. It is particularly important for this client to conscientiously heed the advice of their divination, to avoid serious repercussions and regrets later.

Peculiarity
Ògún, Egúngún, Ọ̀ọ́ṣọ̀ṣì, Ìja, Ọta Rígídí (rocky mountain) and Ewìrì-Mọ̀jé (bellows) are some of the Òrìṣà that speak very loudly in this Odù.
Color
The favorite colors include, but are not limited to, black, red and green.

4

Òdì-Méjí

The fourth of the basic 16 Odù, both in the ancient orthodox and the alternative type of arrangement.

Appearance
The 1st and the 4th lobes of the Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ are open while the 2nd and 3rd are closed, marked on the Ọpọ́n-Ifá.
Pàtàkì (properties)

Òdì-Méjí addresses all aspects of stability in one’s life. The term Òdì means to be blocked, or that the penetrative tip of the arrow is too blunt for an effective breakthrough, and this results in permuting complications in one’s life. Wisdom that comes from maturity is celebrated in this Odù. The ability to form lasting and stable relationships is consecrated in these verses. The ability to reproduce and be proliferative is exalted in Òdì-Méjí.

Forthrightness and honesty are noble characteristics noted in this Odù. Logical reasoning and problem solving are also positive attributes that abound in these verses.

When this Odù comes with Ósọ̀bó, or not in the positive mode, it may mean that an individual’s path is blocked. This blockage may be manifested spiritually, physically, financially or in many other ways. This is commonly referred to as having “one’s hands tied”. However, there is a sacrifice known as “Digging the hole of Òdì-Méjí”, which unties one’s hands and allows for the flow of positive and invigorating blessings and life changes. It is also important to be diligent and uplift one’s Orí, to benefit when timing and opportunities coincide in one’s life.

Peculiarity
Ọbàtálá, Ọbàlùfọ̀n, Egúngún, Dàda, Baáyànì and Ìyàmi are some of the familiar Òrìṣà that speak very loudly in this Odù.
Color
The favorite colors include, but are not limited to, white, black, gray and green.

5

Ìròsùn-Méjì

The fifth of the basic 16 Odù in the alternative arrangement, and the 7th in the ancient orthodox arrangement.

Appearance
The 1st and 2nd lobes of the Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ are open while the 3rd and 4th are closed, marked on the Ọpọ́n-Ifá.
Pàtàkì (properties)

Deep thinkers, absent-mindedness, and the “ability to think the unthinkable or to create the unfathomable reality”. For example, the practicality of quantum physics theory, generated from deep thought, allowed for the inception and creation of the atomic bomb, which was incomprehensible before that time. These clients have been granted opportunities that have the potential for limitless success and prosperity. Listening and being mindful of instruction, as opposed to stubbornness, can prevent later regrets.

Concerns and attributes of Ìròsùn-Méjì include, but are not limited to, inspirational individuals, the gift of heightened intuition, the ability to benefit when timing and opportunities coincide, procreation and fertility, the blessing of having heirs, spiritual elevation and meditation.

Peculiarity
Ọ̀ṣun, Ọ̀sanyìn, Orò and Ìyàmi are a few of the Òrìṣà that speak very loudly in this Odù.
Color
The favorite colors include, but are not limited to, white, black, all shades of red, and green.

6

Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Méjì

The sixth of the basic 16 Odù in the alternative arrangement, and the 8th in the ancient orthodox arrangement.

Appearance
The 1st and 2nd lobes of the Ọ̀pẹ̀lẹ̀ are closed while the 3rd and 4th are open, marked on the Ọpọ́n-Ifá.
Pàtàkì (properties)

Issues of scarcity and economic recession. It speaks to the virtues of improvisation and ingenuity to overcome and become happy, successful and prosperous. Understanding of macro and microeconomics is key to maximizing profit and financial success. Childlessness and issues related to motherhood are also addressed in Ọ̀wọ́nrín-Méjì.

Peculiarity
Ajé, Ògún, Orò, Ìyàmi and Egúngún are a few of the Òrìṣà that speak very loudly in this Odù.
Color
All colors.

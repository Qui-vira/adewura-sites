About the Trust Fund

Adewura Giant Farmers Trust Fund works toward a broad-based agro-economy where healthy, organic food is readily available at an affordable cost.

Founder & Cultivator-in-Chief

Dr. Ifábùkúnmi O. Adéwálé

Dr. Adéwálé is also the medical director of Adewura Natural and the custodian of Ilé Àtègbè.

Our mission

To provide a conducive, healthy and friendly environment that will create a comprehensive, broad-based agro-economy, where quality organic nourishment is readily available for human development at an affordable cost.

Our vision

Sustainable farming in harmony with nature, a world-class agro-economy, and affordable organic food for everyone, with care for people and the planet.

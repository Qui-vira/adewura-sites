About the Trust Fund

We are here to assist cultivators in nurturing thriving and resilient harvests.

We all need support through the crucial stages of our crops: from planting to early growth, ensuring a bountiful harvest ahead.

At Adewura Giant Farmers, we understand the significance of nurturing crops through pivotal stages, akin to the early years of children. Join us as we provide unwavering support, ensuring your agricultural journey yields a flourishing harvest from the very beginning.

Founder & Cultivator-in-Chief

Dr. Ifábùkúnmi O. Adéwálé

As the Founder & Cultivator-in-Chief of Adewura Giant Farmers, I sow the seeds of agricultural innovation, nurturing a vision where every field flourishes with abundance. Join us on this agrarian journey, where growth is not just a process but a promise fulfilled.

Cultivate the harvest of your dreams, for in every seed planted lies the promise of a unique and verdant story, waiting to unfold in the fields of possibility.

Our mission

To provide a conducive, healthy and friendly environment that will create a comprehensive, broad-based agro-economy, where quality organic nourishment is readily available for human development at an affordable cost.

Our vision

To cultivate a thriving future where sustainable agricultural practices harmonize with nature, fostering a world-class agro-economy. We envision a society where every individual has access to high-quality, organic nourishment, promoting holistic human development. Our commitment is to create an inclusive, affordable, and eco-friendly environment that ensures the well-being of both people and the planet.

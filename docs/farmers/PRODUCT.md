# Product: Adewura Giant Farmers Trust Fund

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to support or take part in organic farming, and buyers who want healthy food at an affordable price. Details of the audience still to come from the client.

## Product Purpose

Present the Trust Fund, its mission and its four programs, and let a visitor book a call.

## Positioning

From the old site (adewuragiantfarmers.org, saved 17 Oct 2026): "To provide a conducive, healthy and friendly environment that will create a comprehensive broad-based agro-economy, where all qualitative organic nourishment will be readily available for human development at an affordable cost." The old site calls the organization "Adewura Giant Farmers, Inc". The client calls it Adewura Giant Farmers Trust Fund.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Lives at `/farmers/` under the parent brand on one domain (Option 3).
- Programs named on the old site: Corn Field, Livestock Farming, Poultry Farming, Plantain Farming. No descriptions exist yet.
- Hero object: the 3D maize cob, which fits the Corn Field program.
- Separate from AGFICI (adewuragfici.com), which the client asked us not to touch. No AGFICI words or photos are used here.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: the crown emblem (gold crown, beaded centre, green band with stars), from the old site's IMG_4024-1.png.
- Palette: butter #F7F290, maize #E8B931, cream #FEFAF1, leaf #57772B, olive #403F2B. Chosen because the logo is gold and green.

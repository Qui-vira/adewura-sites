# Product: AGFICI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Donors, partners, volunteers and event attendees who want to see AGFICI's work in rural Nigeria and decide to support it. Women farmers and processors are the people the work serves; the site speaks about them, mostly to supporters.

## Product Purpose

Show that AGFICI does real farming and livelihood work, then move the visitor to get involved, attend an event or donate (presentational only in this build).

## Positioning

Per the current site: Adewura Giant Farmers International Community Initiative is a nonprofit for sustainable agriculture, community development and economic empowerment of women, youth and rural families, with core operations in Nigeria. Named crops: corn, banana fibers, palm, cashew, teak, gmelina, African walnut, bamboo, cane. Signature program: women processing cassava into garri, with training, equipment and market access.

## Operating Context

Events with dates and venues in Abuja and elsewhere in Nigeria. An Instagram feed. Donations and member subscriptions previously ran on Wix.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages in this build: Home, Our Story, Projects (Farming, Empowerment, Community, Garri livelihoods), Events with one event page, Get Involved and Contact.
- Hero object: a 3D maize cob, built kernel by kernel, turning with scroll. Confirmed by the designer.
- The old site's events are dated January 2026 and earlier, so they are now in the past. Ask the client for current events or show them as past events.
- The old legal pages are unedited Wix templates. Do not carry them over. Real policies must come from the client.
- Open: the Restoria product pages on adewuragfici.com. The designer assigns Restoria to the medical brand. Client to confirm whether AGFICI keeps any mention.

## Brand Commitments

- Signature hues kept: butter yellow #F7F290 and olive #403F2B, rebuilt on light grounds.
- Logo: "AGFICI" letterforms with a small jar mark, butter yellow on olive. Keep or redesign is pending the client.

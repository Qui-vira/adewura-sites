How the work grows

Farming

We implement organic farming techniques to promote healthier crops and better yields, while respecting the environment and local ecosystems.

Empowerment

Our programs focus on empowering women through training and resources, so they have the skills and knowledge to thrive in agriculture.

Community

AGFICI works closely with local communities to foster collaboration, build resilience and promote collective efforts for sustainable development.

The work is in the field

Transforming cassava into garri under the sun sustains communities. Garri stands for culture, stability and income, and AGFICI supports the women who process it with training, equipment and market access.
